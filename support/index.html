Your Player ID

Every copy of Tippity has its own Player ID, a code of 12 letters and digits. To find it:

  1. While you play, tap the pause button at the top right.
  2. The Player ID is near the bottom of the panel.
  3. Tap it to copy it, then paste it into your email.

Deleting the game and installing it again makes a new Player ID.

Questions

How do I restart a level?

Tap the pause button, then Restart. Before your first move, the level starts over at once. After it, Restart asks first: the attempt ends and a new one starts, usually with the fruit in a new order.

Can a level be impossible?

No. Every level can be won without a booster, and the same moves always end the same way. A new attempt can bring the fruit in a different order, so a level that went wrong once may go better the next time. If one still seems impossible, write to us with its number.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the pause button: the panel has a switch for sound effects, one for music and one for vibration, and Tippity remembers them. The game also stays quiet while your iPhone is in silent mode.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your iPhone: your levels, gold, boosters and settings. Deleting Tippity deletes them. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, restoring that backup brings them back.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Tippity needs no connection to play. When it is online, it sends us the gameplay records our privacy policy describes.

How do I have my data deleted?

Email us your Player ID and ask us to delete your gameplay records; we answer within 30 days. We delete every record within 90 days of receiving it anyway, and deleting the game deletes what it keeps on your iPhone. The privacy policy has the details.

Which devices does Tippity run on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later.
