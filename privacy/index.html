Tippity Privacy Policy

Effective date

Tippity is a puzzle game for iPhone made by developer's legal name ("we", "us"), who publishes it on the App Store. This policy explains what the game collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

Contact: privacy email address

In short

  • The game has no accounts, no chat, no ads and no in-app purchases. It never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
  • It sends us a record of how you play, under random IDs, so we can see how the levels play and improve the game.
  • It doesn't track you: it never reads your device's advertising identifier, and nothing it sends is linked to data from other companies' apps or websites.
  • We delete the gameplay records within 90 days of receiving them.

What the game collects

Gameplay records

While you play, the game sends us a short record of each event: the game starting, pausing, resuming and closing, and a level or a mode starting, ending or being left. Each record holds:

  • the level, its number and which attempt at it this is;
  • for a level that ended or was left: the moves made and where, the seconds played, the boosters and offers used, the score and the result (won or lost, and why);
  • the time (in UTC), the game's version and the platform (iOS);
  • a random session ID that the game makes each time it starts, and a random install ID that it makes the first time it starts, which the game shows as your Player ID.

The two IDs are random numbers made by the game. They are not linked to your name, your Apple Account, your device's advertising identifier or any other ID of your device, and the records carry no other details about your device.

Your IP address reaches Google's servers with each request, as every request over the internet does; the records we store don't contain it.

On your iPhone

The game keeps your progress, your gold and boosters, your settings and its install ID in its own storage on your iPhone.

Why we use it

  • To see how players play: which levels are too hard or too easy, where players stop, and how long and how often they play, so we can improve the game.

No one pays us for your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who receives it

  • Google runs, on our behalf, the service that receives the gameplay records (Google Apps Script) and the spreadsheet that stores them (Google Sheets).

No one else receives it, unless the law requires us to hand it over.

Where it is stored

Google stores the records on its servers, which can be in countries other than yours, including the United States. The legal basis for transfers abroad, after the legal check: see the KVKK section.

How long we keep it

  • Gameplay records: we delete them within 90 days of receiving them. We may keep totals that identify no player, such as how many players won a level.
  • On your iPhone: the game's progress, settings and install ID stay until you delete the game. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup holds a copy of them too, and restoring it brings them back.

Your choices and rights

  • Deleting the game deletes everything it keeps on your iPhone (but not a backup made before).
  • Web browsers' "Do Not Track" signal doesn't reach apps, so the game doesn't respond to it. The game doesn't track you across apps, so it never asks for iOS's tracking permission.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your gameplay records, ask us to delete them, or object to our use of them, by emailing privacy email address. The records carry only random IDs, so we can find yours only by its Player ID, which the game shows in its pause panel, under its link to this policy; tap it to copy it. Include it in your email. Deleting and installing the game again makes a new Player ID.
  • We answer within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has played it, email us and we will delete the gameplay records we can find.

Security

The game sends its records over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The spreadsheet that stores them is private to us.

Turkish law (KVKK)

developer's legal name is the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The legal basis for each use, after the legal check. Under Article 11 of the KVKK you can ask us whether we process your data and for information about it; learn why we process it and whether it is used for that purpose; learn who receives it in Türkiye and abroad; ask us to correct it, or to delete or anonymize it, and to tell those who received it; object to a result that goes against you and comes only from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. Send requests to privacy email address.

Changes

When this policy changes, we post the new version here with a new effective date.

About this website

This website sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: when you visit, GitHub receives your IP address, as every web server does, and handles it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive it.
