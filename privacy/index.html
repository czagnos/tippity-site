Tippity Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Tippity is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Can Zağnos ("we", "us"), who publishes it on the App Store. This policy explains what the game keeps and what it sends.

Contact: czagnos@gmail.com

In short

  • The game collects no data about you. It sends nothing about you or your play to us or to anyone else.
  • It has no accounts, no chat and no ads. It never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
  • You can buy in-game items, such as gold, with in-app purchases. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.
  • It doesn't track you: it never reads your device's advertising identifier, so it never asks for iOS's tracking permission.

What stays on your iPhone

The game keeps your progress, your gold and boosters, the purchases it has given you, your settings and a log of your play in its own storage on your iPhone. None of it leaves your iPhone.

The pause panel shows a Player ID: a random number the game makes the first time it starts. It stays on your iPhone too, and it is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or any ID of your device.

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup holds a copy of what the game keeps, and restoring it brings it back. Deleting the game deletes everything it keeps on your iPhone (but not a backup made before).

Apple

Apple runs the App Store. If you agreed in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may show us statistics and crash reports about the game that don't identify you. Apple's privacy policy covers them.

In-app purchases are made with Apple: Apple processes the payment and keeps your purchase history with your Apple Account, under Apple's privacy policy. The game only asks the App Store for its prices and for the purchases it has to give you, and tells the App Store once it has given one. Apple shows us sales reports that don't identify you.

Your choices

  • Deleting the game deletes everything it keeps on your iPhone.
  • Web browsers' "Do Not Track" signal doesn't reach apps, so the game doesn't respond to it. The game doesn't track you in any way.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone who plays it, children included.

Turkish law (KVKK)

The game sends us no personal data, so we process none of it under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). If you email us, we use your address and your message only to answer you. Send any request under the KVKK to czagnos@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: czagnos@gmail.com.

Changes

When this policy changes, for example when a version of the game starts sending data, we post the new version here with a new effective date before that version comes out.

About this website

This website sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: when you visit, GitHub receives your IP address, as every web server does, and handles it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive it.
