How it plays

How it plays

One fruit at a time

Every move is a choice between two pans, and every choice moves the needle.

  1. Drop the next fruit

    Fruits come down the line one at a time. Tap the left or the right side to drop the next one into that pan, or hold first to see where the needle will settle.

  2. Match three

    Three of the same fruit in one pan pop, and that pan gets lighter at once. The needle swings the other way.

  3. Keep it level

    Bigger fruits weigh more. Keep the needle out of the red while you reach each level's goal, most often a scale with every pan empty.

What's inside

Easy to learn, hard to keep level

  • Always something new

    Each chapter adds its own twist to the balance, from gentle warm-ups to tricky peaks. More levels are on the way.

  • Boosters for close calls

    When the needle edges into the red, a well-timed booster can turn the level around.

  • More ways to play

    Bonus rounds for gold, and more to unlock as you go.

  • Fair, and yours to solve

    No luck: the same moves always end the same way. Plays offline, with one thumb.
